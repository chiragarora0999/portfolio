    I started in Computer Science, specialising in AI & Machine Learning. Then I spent nearly five years growing from intern to Principal Analyst at a global SaaS company. Now I'm doing a Master of Commerce at UNSW in Sydney and building my own AI products on the side.

    Live

    Visual Product Finder

    Solo Founder & Product Manager · 2026

    A live Chrome extension (Manifest V3). Click any image or paused video frame on a webpage and it uses AI vision to identify the product, then gives you real Amazon/Flipkart search links.

    • Directed the whole build with AI-assisted development: the extension, an Express/TypeScript backend on Render, and the landing page.
    • Region-aware affiliate system across 3 Amazon marketplaces (US, India, Australia), including W-8BEN registration, plus Cuelinks & Impact.com.
    • Ran Google Ads (search + video) and made launch content for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram & LinkedIn.
    • Diagnosed funnel issues from live analytics and shipped fixes. It only ever shows real marketplace results.
